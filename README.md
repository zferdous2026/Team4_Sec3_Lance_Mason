# Team4_Sec3_Lance_Mason
## Participant ID: The ID assigned to each participant to identify them across tables. 
## Treatment group (Char) : whether patients regularly consume or avoid peanuts in their daily lives.
## Visit: The visit number the patient is on in for this study.
## Date of last follow up: The date of the patient’s final visit
## Age (Months) : The patient’s age in month 
## Sex  (char) : the patient’s sex 
## Date of study day 0: The day of the first consultation with the patient 
## Date of first peanut challenge: The day the fist peanut challenge was administered to determine if a patient is allergic to peanuts.
## Last recorded visit date : The date of the patient’s final visit.
## Follow up time in months: the time between the patients first and last visit 
## As treated population: What treatment group the patient actually took part in between peanut consumption or peanut avoidance.
## Allergens skin prick test date: The date the skin test was administered and the wheal was checked.
## Positive control Wheal (mm): a reaction of the positive control skin test indicated the infant’s skin is capable of responding to an allergen. 
## Negative control wheal (mm): A skin prick test is conducted with no allergen to set a baseline skin reaction.
## Type of birth: Whether the patient was born vaginally or via C-section
